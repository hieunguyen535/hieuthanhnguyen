Welcome to my homepage!

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Wisconsin–Madison .

My advisor is Professor Jean-Luc Thiffeault and my informal advisor is Professor Franz Gmeineder .

Broadly speaking, my research interests are in pure and applied aspects of partial differential equations and fluid mechanics.

I am a big fan of running and watching football. I enjoy spending time with friends, and exploring all the different restaurants and coffee shops that Madison has to offer. I love all things mathematics and getting to know my peers outside of class.
